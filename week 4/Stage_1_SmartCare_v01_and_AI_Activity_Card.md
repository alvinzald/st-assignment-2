*A Case Study Stage 1 student resource*

## SmartCare Scenario

------------

SmartCare Community Clinic currently uses spreadsheets and paper records to manage patients and appointments.
The client says: 'We need software to help manage patients, practitioners and appointments.'
This is not yet a complete specification

## Initial Engineering Brief

------------

1. **Problem summary**

    SmartCare is a small community clinic that currently manages patient information and appointments using spreadsheets, paper records, and manual processes. This caused problems in the clinic such as duplicate bookings, difficulty finding records, and inconsistent appointment status. The clinic also does not have a reliable appointment history and finds it difficult to produce basic operational reports. SmartCare needs an application that can solve these issues while remaining simple and appropriate for the small clinic.


2. **Initial stakeholders**
    
    | Stakeholder | Possible need |
    |:---|:---|
    | Reception | Prevent duplicate appointment bookings.<br> Access patient records easily.<br> Maintain consistent appointment status information.<br> View practitioner availability.<br> Automate cancellation process.<br> View reliable appointment history. |
    | GPs | Access patient records easily.<br> Have consistent appointment status information.<br> View their availability and appointments.<br> View reliable appointment history. |
    | Management | Have reliable appointment history.<br> Produce basic operational reports.<br> Have a simple system appropriate for the clinic. |
    | Patients | Prevent duplicate appointment bookings.<br> Have consistent appointment information.<br> Cancel appointments.


3. **Initial features**
    
    | Feature | Confirmed or provisional? | Why? |
    |:---|:---|:---|
    | Appointment management | Confirmed | To create, manage, and cancel appointments. |
    | Duplicate booking prevention | Confirmed | To prevent practitioners from being double booked. |
    | Patient management | Confirmed | To find, store, and change patient records easily instead of relying on spreadsheets and paper records. |
    | GP management | Confirmed | To store GP details and appointment availability. |
    | Basic operational reporting | Confirmed | To create operational reports for management to use and analyse. |


4. Questions for client

   1. Who are the users of the system? If the patient are also users of the system, can they also manage their appointments with the application or just view?
   2. What patient information needs to be stored by the system?
   3. What appointment information needs to be collected by the system? Are there pre-determined duration for each appointment such as 15-minute short consultations? What about reasons for booking/cancellation?
   4. What information do we keep for each health practitioner?
   5. What kind of operational reports does the management want the system to produce?
   6. Assuming the system can be used by different types of users, who can create the accounts for login? Can each patient create their account or does the admin staff have to create it for them?


5. What do we not yet know

   1. User access requirements
   2. Patient information requirements
   3. GP information requirements
   4. Booking specifics
   5. Operational report requirements
